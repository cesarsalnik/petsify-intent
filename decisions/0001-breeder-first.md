# 0001: Breeders are the first paying customer

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-09-01

## Context

Petsify serves three groups: breeders, adopters and buyers. All three have real pain, but only one has both a business problem and a budget. Trying to monetise everyone at once would spread a solo founder too thin.

## Decision

Breeders are our first target paying segment; we are testing whether they will pay for saved time and better inquiries. Adopters and buyers are the demand side: they create value for breeders through traffic and good inquiries, but we don't charge them. Adoption stays an open, free channel.

## Why

- Breeders have a business problem we can measure (inquiry quality, time spent answering), so whether they'll pay for solving it is a question we can actually test.
- Proof of demand from paying breeders is the strongest argument for investors and the cheapest path to revenue.
- Adopters bring traffic and trust. Charging them would cut both.

## Consequences

- Product priorities follow the breeder's day: qualifying questionnaire, profile FAQ, business stats.
- Marketing copy talks to breeders about time and lead quality, not "visibility".
- Open question we still test: does having adoption listings next to breeder listings make the platform less attractive to breeders? (See the brief, assumption 3.)

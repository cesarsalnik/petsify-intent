# 0004: Four features cut from the MVP

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-08-06

## Context

Ideas grow faster than a solo founder can ship them. By August there was a list of attractive features that didn't serve the first paying customer.

## Decision

Out of MVP scope:

- cost-of-ownership calculator,
- mascot,
- full health profile for pets,
- marketplace for pet services.

## Why

None of them helps a breeder get better inquiries or save time, which is what we're trying to prove first ([0001](0001-breeder-first.md)). Each would take build time away from the one thing we need to learn first: whether breeders pay.

## Consequences

- These stay in the backlog as later-stage ideas, mainly for the "take care of your pet for life" part of the mission.
- Before reopening any of them, check that breeder demand is proven.
